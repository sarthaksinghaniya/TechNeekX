# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Student Builders & Innovators:** Technical creators seeking collaborative hackathons, real AI project development, mentorship, and community visibility.
- **Ecosystem & Academic Partners:** Tech companies, university incubators, and organizers seeking high-velocity engineering talent and community hackathon partnerships.
- **Evaluators & Recruiters:** Engineering leads evaluating real shipped projects, systems architecture, and community leadership.

## Product Purpose

TechNeekX (TNX) exists to transform ambitious builder talent into production-grade engineering excellence. It provides a launchpad for real AI products across healthcare, sustainability, fintech, and education while anchoring a nationwide youth builder collective. Success means active hackathon participation, high-impact deployed systems, and seamless builder onboarding.

## Positioning

Unlike traditional student clubs or speculative agencies, TechNeekX is an active AI Technology Studio and Builder Ecosystem with a proven track record of shipping live, functional AI systems (e.g., Hospital Pulse, Fracture Vision AI, Hanu Youth) backed by over 80 hackathon milestones.

## Operating Context

- Central web portal acting as the single source of truth for TechNeekX initiatives, active events, and portfolio showcases.
- Embedded within fast-paced hackathons, open-source building, community sprints, and partner collaborations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:** Project showcase with live demos and repository links, hackathon event management and registration gateways, initiative spotlights (Girls in TechNeekX), partner ecosystem registry, and interactive team timeline.
- **Constraints:** Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS and CSS modules. All project and event data must be backed by structured datasets (`data/`).
- **Terminology:** TechNeekX, TNX, Builders, AI Studio, Ecosystem Partners.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** TechNeekX (TNX).
- **Voice & Tone:** Ambitious, technical, disciplined, enterprise-grade, builder-centric.
- **Visual Stance:** Enterprise-grade AI & Technology Studio aesthetic—clean neutrals, graphite typography, architectural grid accents, and high-craft component surfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Live Deployed Projects:** Hospital Pulse (`hopx.netlify.app`), Fracture Vision AI (`fracturevision-ai.streamlit.app`), Revibe Lab (`revibe-lab.netlify.app`), Hanu Youth (`hanuyouthapp.netlify.app`), Hanu Planner (`hanuplanner.netlify.app`).
- **Verified Milestones:** 80+ hackathons, 15+ AI builds, documented in `data/events.json` and `data/data.json`.
- **Roster & Partners:** Verified team profiles in `data/team.json` and ecosystem partners in `data/community_partners.json`. Future copy must not fabricate unverified corporate clients.

## Product Principles

1. **Ship Real Artifacts:** Prioritize live, functional deployments over conceptual placeholders.
2. **Builder-Centric Gravity:** Foreground the engineers, projects, and actual problem-solving milestones.
3. **Engineering Craft:** Maintain production rigor, clean performance, and crisp visual hierarchy on every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adherence to WCAG 2.1 AA standards for contrast ratios, semantic heading hierarchies, responsive scaling across mobile/desktop, and keyboard navigation.
